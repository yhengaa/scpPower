Designing a two-condition single-cell proteomics experiment

Start with the carrier-TMT Leduc preset and ask whether increasing biological samples helps detect a twofold change at a fixed cell budget. These are planning estimates under the preset assumptions, within the limited validation range reported below. The example uses 20261013 as its recorded random seed.

The matrix has 3433 proteins and 128 cells. Biological-sample, acquisition-batch and cell noise are simulated on the log2 scale; missing values remain missing. Joint resampling retains each protein’s abundance, variance and detection parameters.

These demonstration runs use 20 replicates; increase that argument for a more precise estimate and read simulation_se beside power. Instrument hours count occupied batches using the preset’s 95 minutes per run and exclude unreported overhead.

Unconditional power divides detected changed proteins by all changed proteins. Testability counts proteins with enough quantified cells in every biological sample for the registered endpoint. Conditional power divides detected changed proteins by changed proteins that are testable within a replicate; it is undefined in replicates with none. Missingness can therefore cap unconditional power even when conditional power is high. Per-sample means are tested with limma-trend, and sign agreement is required for a changed protein’s detection. Read realised FDR beside rejection counts to recognise nearly empty tests:

Before choosing a design, retain these limitations:

  1. Between-donor variance is mapped from whole-PBMC bulk data. Its transfer to single cells is an assumption that requires a pilot in your own cell type.
  2. Compression defaults to 1; the sensitivity assumptions are 0.75 and 0.5. Neither donor transfer nor compression is validated by the semi-synthetic exercise.
  3. Real-cell carrier-TMT validation reaches observed power of about 0.36; it does not establish accuracy above that range.
  4. Cell-depth dependence is omitted: observed depth SD is 5.17 to 7.50 times the independent simulation’s SD. This did not affect the registered validation verdict; correlated missingness remains a limitation.
  5. Q Exactive and timsTOF public cells cannot validate designs at useful power.
  6. The primary endpoint uses per-sample means and limma-trend. Other tests can give different power estimates.
