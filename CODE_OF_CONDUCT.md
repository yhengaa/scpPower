# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss evidence and proposed changes clearly. Criticism should address the work, and disagreements should leave room for correction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, publication of private information and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Youshan Heng, at poggar@hotmail.com. Include the relevant links and context. Reports will be handled confidentially where possible; information will be shared only as needed to assess and address the concern.

The maintainer may remove content, request a correction, restrict participation or exclude a participant according to the conduct and its impact. Reports involving the maintainer can be directed to GitHub through its platform reporting tools. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is unacceptable.
